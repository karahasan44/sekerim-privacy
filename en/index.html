Glucose Log (Şekerim) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Türkçe sürüm · English

In short: Glucose Log never connects to the internet. The blood sugar readings, alarms and settings you enter stay on your phone and are never sent to us or to any third party. The app does not request the INTERNET permission. Android does not allow an app without this permission to send data over the network.
Glucose Log is not a medical device. It must not be used to diagnose, to decide on treatment or to choose medication or insulin doses. Review your readings and your medication plan with your doctor. Reminders can be late or fail to go off because of phone settings, battery saving or missing permissions.

Information stored by the app

Glucose Log has no server, user account or cloud service. What you enter is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Blood sugar readings: date, time, fasting or after-meal type, value in mg/dL and an optional note.
  • Alarms: reminder type (diabetes medication or insulin), alarm time, repeat pattern, alert type and the alarm sound you chose.
  • Settings: language, theme, alert preferences and the blood sugar ranges you consider normal.

Readings are health data. They never reach us; only you can see, change or delete them.

Permissions and what they are used for

All of the permissions below are used to make reminders go off on time and to export readings. None of them collects personal data.

The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call history or photos.

Voice alerts

Alarm messages are read aloud by the text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed on your phone. This is an Android system service, usually Google's Speech Services or the phone maker's own engine. The only text read aloud is a fixed reminder sentence such as "Time to take your diabetes medication"; your readings are never read out. How the engine processes data is covered by the privacy policy of the company that provides it.

Exporting to Excel

"Export" in Settings saves the readings from the date range you choose as an Excel file in your phone's Downloads folder. This only happens when you tap the button. Once saved, the file can be opened by other apps on your phone, and you decide who you share it with. Glucose Log does not send the file anywhere.

Android automatic backup

On supported phones, Android can back up app data to your own Google account. Glucose Log does not turn this system feature off, so your readings and settings may be included in your Google account backup, depending on your Android settings. This backup is kept on Google's infrastructure and tied to your account; neither Glucose Log nor we have access to it. You can manage backups under Settings → Google → Backup on your phone (menu names vary by device).

Sharing with third parties

None. The app contains no ads, no analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), no crash reporting and no other tracking library. Since no data is collected, no data is sold, rented or shared.

Children's privacy

Glucose Log collects no data from anyone and is not marketed to any particular age group.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete a single reading or alarm with its delete button in the list.
  • "Delete old readings" in Settings deletes every reading before the date you choose.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. You can delete the automatic backup in your Google account from your Google settings.

Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. A significant change, such as adding a network connection or a third-party service, will also be mentioned in the release notes.
